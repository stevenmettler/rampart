Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 19, 2026

Rampart is an accountability app. That means it handles some of the most personal information there is — a person's recovery journey. We take that seriously. This page explains what we collect, why, who can see it, and how you can delete it, in plain language.

What we collect

DataWhat it's for
Email addressSign-in, and how your accountability partner is invited/identified.
Covenant textThe personal commitment you write for yourself in the app. Visible only to you.
Daily check-insYour "doing well" / "struggling" status, sent to your accountability partner.
Website whitelistThe list of sites you're allowed to visit, set by your partner and stored so it can sync to your device.
Site requestsDomains you've asked your partner to allow.
Device push tokenLets us notify you or your partner (e.g. "struggling" alerts, whitelist updates). Apple-issued, not personally identifying on its own.
Subscription statusWhether you're on a free trial or paid plan. We never see or store your card number — that's handled directly by Stripe or Apple.

Who can see your data

Your accountability partner — the person you invite — can see your website whitelist, your site requests, and your check-in / "struggling" alerts. That's the whole point of the app: they're the one holding you accountable. They cannot see your password, your payment information, or anything you haven't explicitly shared through the app.

We (Rampart) can access data only as needed to operate and support the app — for example, to debug a sync issue you report. We do not read your covenant text or check-ins as a matter of course.

We never sell your data. Not to advertisers, not to data brokers, not to anyone. There is no third-party analytics or ad tracking in this app.

Sensitive data

Rampart is built around a Christian, faith-based approach to recovery from pornography. Because of that, some of what you enter — your covenant text, your check-ins, the fact that you use this app at all — can reveal information about your religious beliefs and your mental/behavioral health. We treat all of it as sensitive data, restrict access to it accordingly, and will never use it for advertising or share it with anyone outside your accountability partnership.

Data retention

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. If you dissolve a partnership, the whitelist and history tied to that partnership are removed. If you delete your account (see below), everything tied to you is permanently deleted.

Deleting your data

You can permanently delete your account, and everything tied to it, at any time — directly in the app. Open Rampart, tap the settings icon (the gear, in the top corner of the Today or Partner tab), and tap Delete Account. This immediately and permanently removes your profile, covenant text, check-in history, website whitelist, and partnership records. It cannot be undone. If you're an accountability partner for someone else, deleting your account safely ends that partnership and notifies their device — it doesn't leave them stuck with restrictions they can no longer manage.

If you'd rather not use the in-app flow, or you have a broader data request, email privacy@rampartaccountability.com and we'll handle it directly.

Your rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Wherever you live, you can ask us at any time to: access the data we hold about you, correct it, export it, or delete it. The fastest way to access, correct, or delete most of it is directly in the app. For anything else, email privacy@rampartaccountability.com and we'll respond within 30 days.

Children's privacy (COPPA)

Rampart is not directed at children and is not intended for anyone under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us at privacy@rampartaccountability.com and we'll delete it.

How we secure your data

Your data is stored with Supabase (PostgreSQL), protected by row-level security policies so that only you and your accountability partner can access what's relevant to your partnership. Passwords are never stored in plain text. Push notifications are sent directly through Apple's APNs service — we don't route your data through third-party marketing or analytics platforms.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, notify you in the app.

Questions about this policy or your data? Email privacy@rampartaccountability.com.
